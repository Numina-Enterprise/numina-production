SUPPLY CHAIN / USE CASES

Connect payment decisions
to operational reality.

Illustrative implementations, scoped around your systems and counterparties.

Manufacturers: release by delivery milestone

Match supplier invoices to purchase orders and accepted goods receipts. Route quantity variances to procurement and prepare payment only for approved deliveries. A warehouse event is evidence for review, not automatic proof that every contractual condition is met.

Distributors: capture supplier discounts

Surface invoices eligible for early-payment discounts alongside cash availability and due dates. Calculate the discount against funding and payment costs, then let treasury authorize the schedule.

Importers: compare cross-border routes

Connect landed-cost records, supplier currency requirements, and approved beneficiary details. Compare wire and eligible digital payout routes, track quote expiry, and preserve customs and invoice references for reconciliation.

Logistics teams: reconcile freight invoices

Match carrier invoices to contracted rates, shipment identifiers, and proof of delivery. Flag duplicate surcharges, disputed accessorial fees, and missing documents before the payment run.

Multi-location businesses: coordinate cash

Consolidate upcoming obligations by entity and currency. Prepare internal funding requests and supplier batches without mixing ownership, account permissions, or operating reserves.

Procurement: protect supplier changes

Send changed beneficiary details through an independent verification workflow. Place related invoices on hold until a designated reviewer approves the change through a trusted channel.
