01 — MY STORY

Practical experience.
Forward-looking curiosity.

I build technology that connects software engineering with the realities of running complex operations.

I'm an operations-focused transportation management system (TMS) developer with a background in logistics technology. My work includes designing and implementing infrastructure for private fleet operations supporting brands such as Procter & Gamble and John Deere.

At CPC Logistics, I work closely with business stakeholders and operations teams to turn day-to-day requirements into dependable tools and integrations. That means thinking beyond individual features: how systems support execution, improve efficiency, meet compliance requirements, and scale across transportation networks.

Alongside that work, I founded SK Consulting to help organizations with software, information security, electronic data interchange (EDI), database administration, infrastructure, and business development. Emerging technology and the practical work of integrating it into existing products are central to that practice.

As Founder at Numina Labs, I bring that systems perspective to technology for future-state financial markets. Our focus on AI-native solutions connects my interest in emerging technology with the challenges of complexity, uncertainty, and adaptation.

Whether I'm working on an internal transformation or helping develop a new venture, I value action, thoughtful systems design, and work that creates lasting value.

03 — BEYOND THE WORK
EDUCATION & COMMUNITY

Investing in
the next generation.

Access to technology can change a student's future.

In 2025, I established the Kott Family Fund for STEM & Emerging Technology in Education in Honor of Gary D. Jewel through the Community Foundation of the Fox River Valley. I support its mission to expand educational opportunity.

Named in honor of my grandfather, the fund carries forward the Jewel and Kott families' commitment to public education and community service. As a graduate of West Aurora School District 129, I want students at West Aurora Schools to have access to learning opportunities in STEM, artificial intelligence, and digital education, regardless of background.

My interest in emerging technology took shape during my time at the University of Iowa, where I explored cryptocurrency and its real-world applications. That experience continues to inform how I evaluate new ideas: with curiosity, conviction, and an appreciation for timing and persistence.

I believe students should have the opportunity to understand technology, build with it, and help shape the world it creates.
