KOTTS LAW / A STATED PRINCIPLE

A linear, horizontal progression
on an eternal upward trajectory.

“Every distinct object carries one singular identity, advancing in equal linear steps along a horizontal progression — rising forever, never falling, never reversing. An eternal upward trajectory.”

Stated by Samuel J. Kott, November 19, 2026.

THE LAW / FOUR CLAUSES

Four clauses. One trajectory.

Distinct objects.

Every object in the progression is distinct from every other.

Singular identities.

Each object carries one singular identity, and only one.

Horizontal and linear progression.

The progression moves horizontally, in equal linear steps.

Eternal upward trajectory.

The trajectory climbs upward forever — never decreasing, never reversing, no ceiling.

WHAT IT IS

A named principle of the Numina work.

Kotts Law is Samuel J. Kott’s own named principle for how distinct objects progress: each object carries one singular identity, the progression moves horizontally in equal linear steps, and the trajectory climbs upward forever.

In Samuel J. Kott’s words, Murphy’s law has run its course, and Newton’s laws keep things in motion; Kotts Law is the progression that motion follows — upward, for eternity.

WHAT IT CARRIES

The people in his life.

In Samuel J. Kott’s words, Kotts Law is the manifest of the people in his life: his family — his twin sister, his older sister, his parents, his father, his mother — Lauren, Bean, and Frank, her family and his family.

What the law carries for them, in his words, is safety, health, wealth, and freedom — financial freedom, labor freedom, legal freedom — a life with no stress, no debt, no hate, no evil, no darkness, no hell.

WHAT IT IS NOT

Not a claim about Newtonian mechanics.

Newton’s three laws of motion stand as they are. Kotts Law is a stated principle of the Numina work, labelled as such everywhere it appears.

IMMUTABLE

It does not change.

Kotts Law does not change, and neither does any state it has already produced. The statement is frozen. Every step returns a new frozen progression; the previous progression is never rewritten, never mutated, never re-pointed. History is preserved exactly as it happened, for eternity.
