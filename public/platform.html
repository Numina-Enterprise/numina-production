NUMINA / PLATFORM

A workspace for accountable agent workflows.

Numina brings service intake, delegated authority and delivery evidence into a common operating workflow. Business owners define the outcome; operators review the permissions, work records and decisions required to deliver it.

Open Numina OSReview engagement options
01 / THE WORKSPACE

Give each request an owner and an acceptance standard.

Use Numina OS to prepare a service brief and return to an existing request. Keep scope, payment evidence and delivery review associated with the work they describe. Payment, engagement acceptance and completion are distinct decisions.

Business owner

Defines the objective, constraints and reviewer. Agrees what successful delivery looks like before implementation begins.

Operator

Reviews access requirements and dependencies, prepares the work and documents exceptions or external handoffs.

Reviewer

Checks the delivered output against the brief and supporting records, then identifies acceptance or further work.

02 / CONTROL & EVIDENCE

Connect permissions to the actions they allow.

The control workspace organizes delegated agents, policy decisions, approvals and journal records. Access to an owner operation requires the corresponding authority; discovering a tool does not grant permission to use it.

Before execution
Define the intended action, permitted systems and required approvals.
At review
Inspect the decision, applicable policy and available execution evidence.
After delivery
Reconcile the reported result with source records and the acceptance criteria.
Open the control workspace →
03 / ADOPTION

Start with one bounded workflow.

A first project should have an identified owner, authorized access to its systems and representative evaluation cases. Scope integrations around the actual environment and document the operating responsibilities before rollout.

  • Choose a recurring task with observable inputs and outputs.
  • Identify sensitive data and approval requirements.
  • Agree evaluation cases, exception handling and handover requirements.
  • Confirm provider access and deployment requirements during scoping.

The web workspace is available through Numina OS. Native distribution, individual provider integrations and financial capabilities have separate availability records.
