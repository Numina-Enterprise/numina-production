01 / WORKFLOW

Connect the brief, the action and the result.

Define the work

Capture the objective, participating systems, constraints and acceptance criteria in a structured service request.

Control the action

Review permissions, policy decisions and approvals in the authorized control workspace.

Verify the outcome
