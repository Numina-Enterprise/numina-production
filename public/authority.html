Identify the actor

Authenticated identity establishes the actor for an internal record. Customer service records stay scoped to their requester and Numina’s operator.

Limit authority

Use explicit internal record permissions and revocable delegation. Credentials do not grant control over unrelated external systems.

Require evidence

Review attestations, conflict resolution and permitted next actions form the evidence basis for committing an internal record.

Preserve the record

Keep revision history, delivery activity and payment evidence available for review. A recorded decision does not itself execute an external transaction.
