OPERATIONAL ENFORCEMENT

A boundary for each action.

Identify the actor

Require a signed-in identity. Customer service records stay scoped to their requester and Numina’s operator.

Limit authority

Use explicit internal record permissions and revocable delegation. Credentials do not grant control over unrelated external systems.

Require evidence

Attach a review attestation, resolve conflicting evidence, and evaluate the allowed next action before a record is committed.

Preserve the record

Keep revision history, delivery activity and payment evidence available for review. A recorded decision does not itself execute an external transaction.

COMMERCIAL RIGHTS

Agree rights before delivery.

Client inputs, background software, deliverables and any license terms belong in the written scope. Report suspected misuse with the work or mark involved and the evidence supporting the claim.
