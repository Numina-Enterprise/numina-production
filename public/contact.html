Common questions.

How do I get help with an existing request?

Sign in to Numina OS to view your service requests and delivery notes. You can also email skott34@numinalabs.xyz with a brief description of the issue and your request reference, if available. Numina OS · Contact and support

What do the diagnostic and audit cost?

The Operational Agent Diagnostic is a fixed fee of $5,000 USD. The Agentic Systems & Operations Audit is a fixed fee of $25,000 USD. Implementation programs are priced against a written scope. Service scope and pricing

Does submitting a brief book an engagement?

Submitting a brief saves a request for review. Payment and engagement acceptance are separate steps. Scope, scheduling, authorized access and confidentiality are confirmed in writing before work begins. Review the published service terms for payment and refund conditions; an FAQ answer does not confirm an account action. Numina OS · Service scope and pricing

Can teams outside the United States inquire?

Yes. Include your operating country and proposed workflow. International requests, country eligibility and payment availability are reviewed before checkout or engagement acceptance. Contact and support · Service scope and pricing

What information should I share first?

Describe the workflow, systems, constraints and desired outcome at a high level. Do not send credentials, payment-card numbers, personal datasets or confidential source material. Agree a suitable transfer method and confidentiality terms before sharing sensitive materials. Contact and support · Privacy notice

How do I request account deletion?

Email skott34@numinalabs.xyz with the subject “Delete my Numina account”, using the email associated with Numina when possible. Numina will reply with any ownership-verification steps and the request outcome. Some payment or security records may need to be retained; sending an email does not instantly delete records.
