CUSTOMER REVIEW

Questions to resolve before implementation.

Data access
Identify the records needed for the workflow, the authorized users and where sensitive information will be handled.
Action authority
Agree which operations may proceed automatically and which require an explicit reviewer decision.
Failure handling
Define how failed requests, uncertain outcomes and external-provider errors are investigated and reconciled.
Operational ownership
Assign responsibility for credentials, access reviews, incident escalation and release acceptance.

Confirm environment-specific requirements in the implementation scope. Published documentation is not a substitute for an independent assessment or a certification.
