READ THIS FIRST

What this page is — and is not.

This page documents a testnet experiment, not a product, a signal, or a promise of returns.

  • Testnet only. Every token named here is a worthless testnet token on Robinhood Chain testnet (chain 46630). Nothing here is real money, real trading, or an offer.
  • Dry-run only. The bot has never placed a live trade. Its wallet is unfunded, and funding it is a separate human step.
  • Blocked on the public RPC. The dry-run stops at the same step twice: the public testnet RPC answers HTTP 429 when the bot asks for block timestamps. No candles, no signal, nothing invented.
  • Mainnet never. The bot refuses any chain other than 46630. No real funds, no owner keys, no seed phrases.
I / THE NETWORK

Robinhood Chain testnet.

Chain 46630. All three endpoints below were verified live while building the bot.

  • RPC: https://rpc.testnet.chain.robinhood.com
  • Explorer: https://explorer.testnet.chain.robinhood.com
  • Faucet: https://faucet.testnet.chain.robinhood.com

The faucet serves a security checkpoint to programmatic fetches, so claiming is a human step in a browser. One claim sends test ETH plus 5 each of the test stock tokens, once per 24 hours.

Two wallets. One is never touched.

Owner wallet (never touch): 0xB1590DE7FA68b4D9Fa4109d1ae0E4AcCb589daeD — faucet-funded with 0.01 test ETH and 5 test TSLA, verified. No sends from it, no signing with it, ever.

Bot wallet (testnet only): 0x76B5CDb9BBC33996baB60f1f3a487676998ECB02 — unfunded: 0 test ETH, 0 WETH, 0 TSLA, verified November 17, 2026, at block 154,550,904. Its key is stored separately, labeled testnet only, and is never printed or logged. Live trading is impossible until the owner funds it.

IV / THE STRATEGY

The strategy, adapted from the owner’s quant bot.

The bot adapts the owner’s quant-bot strategy (EMA, RSI, MACD, ADX, ATR, Choppiness, OBV) to on-chain Swap-event candles. Candles come from Swap events — 1-hour OHLCV, TSLA per WETH — not from an exchange API.

  • Regime: enter only when ADX(14) is above 25 and Choppiness(14) is below 38.2.
  • Long: EMA(9) crosses above EMA(21), RSI below 70, MACD above signal.
  • Exit: opposite crossover, a stop at 2× ATR, or a target at 3× ATR.
  • Sizing: risk 1% of equity per trade.
  • No shorts on a spot AMM. A SHORT signal means exit any open long to ETH and stay flat — never a short sale. Entry window 10.
V / DRY-RUN RESULTS

Blocked twice at the same step.

November 16, 2026. The 7-day Swap-log fetch completed. Then the public RPC returned HTTP 429 on a batched request for 500 block timestamps. The step was stopped, recorded, and not retried.

November 17, 2026, ~08:35 CST — re-run with 25-call batches (owner-authorized). Connected: chain 46630, block 154,550,904. Bot wallet still 0 / 0 / 0. Live quote simulated, read-only: 0.001 WETH → 1.100198 TSLA at block 154,550,930 (spot 1,103.5468 TSLA/WETH, tick 70066). The 7-day Swap-log fetch completed again. Then HTTP 429 again, at batch 25, in the same timestamp step. The public RPC rate-limits even 25-call batches — the limit is on the endpoint generally, not the batch size.

Nothing invented. No candles file, no signal, no “would trade” line came out of either run.

November 17, 2026, ~09:00 CST — alternate RPC attempt (owner-authorized). Three keyless public endpoints were tested once each. BlockVectra read the chain ID, then failed on transport. dRPC timed out after 60 seconds. PublicNode timed out after 60 seconds. No endpoint passed both tests, so the dry-run was not retried. For reference, the earlier verified quote was 0.001 WETH → 1.1012069647550888 TSLA (block 154,377,347).

VI / HARD LIMITS

Hard limits.

  • Testnet only — chain 46630, asserted in code. No mainnet calls.
  • Max 0.001 test ETH per trade.
  • Max 5 live trades total across runs.
  • Stop on any error. No silent retry, no fallback.
  • Never touch the owner’s wallet.
  • Never print or log the bot private key.
  • --live never run. Live trading needs a funded wallet and the owner’s go-ahead.
VII / FUNDING — A HUMAN STEP

Funding is a human step.

The bot wallet holds nothing. To fund it, the owner must do one of these, in a browser:

  • Faucet: open faucet.testnet.chain.robinhood.com, enter the bot address 0x76B5CDb9BBC33996baB60f1f3a487676998ECB02, complete the challenge, and claim. One claim is test ETH plus 5 each of TSLA, AMZN, PLTR, NFLX and AMD.
  • Or send from the owner wallet 0xB1590DE7FA68b4D9Fa4109d1ae0E4AcCb589daeD a little test ETH and one test stock token to the bot address (testnet only, worthless).

Funding the bot wallet does not fix the HTTP 429. That is a separate, still-blocked step.

VIII / WHAT WOULD UNBLOCK IT

What would unblock it.

  • A keyed RPC endpoint. Alchemy is Robinhood’s recommended provider; Chainstack free tier, QuickNode and thirdweb are documented alternatives. The owner supplies the endpoint, then the dry-run is retried once against it. The Alchemy key is pending from the owner.
  • Or a slower approach to the same step — one block at a time with delays, or a shorter candle window needing fewer timestamp lookups.

Either one is a new action on the same failed step and needs the owner’s explicit authorization naming it.
