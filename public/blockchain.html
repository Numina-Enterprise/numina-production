NUMINA / BLOCKCHAIN

The Numina native network.
NUSD as the value unit.

Numina is SK Consulting LLC’s permissioned blockchain network for compute-to-value services. NUSD means Numina USD; its declared valuation is 1 NUSD = 1 USDC = 1 USD, with Numina Treasury as the intended backing source. Token issuance is not equivalent to cash reserves, market capitalization, redemption liquidity, or guaranteed asset value. NUSD issuance is receipt-verified at 18-decimal precision. The operator confirms NUSD is active; that declaration is separate from dated chain observations and application checkout readiness. Redemption and externally funded payment services require separate activation evidence.
