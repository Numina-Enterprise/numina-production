The development program

Founded by Samuel J. Kott, Numina Labs is developing a technology ecosystem spanning cryptographic infrastructure, distributed computing, autonomous agents, neural-network research, advanced reasoning, and independently governed artificial intelligence. The program fits together in eight directions.

Numina Core
The foundation: computational systems whose operations, identities, decisions, and outputs can be independently verified. Ed25519 signatures, RFC 8785 canonical JSON, domain-separated signing, and dual-authority approval policies. The principle: intelligence must be accountable to evidence.
Infrastructure
The computational backbone: cloud services, local computing environments, distributed databases, networking, and security controls — built to support increasingly sophisticated workloads while retaining measurable operational controls.
Agent Architecture
Coordinated intelligence: reasoning, engineering, verification, research, operational, and governance agents organized with separation of duties. No agent’s declaration of success should substitute for independently verifiable evidence.
Numina-NN
The proposed neural-network foundation: representation learning, graph-based intelligence, persistent contextual memory, multimodal intelligence, adaptive reasoning, and scientific discovery support. These are research and development objectives, not claims that a fully trained proprietary neural foundation model has already been completed.
Numina-LLM
An independent language-intelligence platform: natural-language understanding and generation, advanced reasoning, retrieval-augmented knowledge access, persistent memory, structured tool use, and cryptographic provenance. A central design objective is to distinguish what a model predicts, what a system has verified, and what an authorized operator has approved.
The Singularity Protocol
Research into pair-binding relationships, cryptographically attributable node associations, graph-based representations, distributed consensus resilience, and information provenance. These investigations are exploratory; they do not establish a completed theory of quantum gravity or demonstrated artificial superintelligence.
Numina Trust
Cryptographically governed intelligence: identity, authorization, execution, evidence, verification, attestation. The objective is intelligent software whose consequential actions can be inspected, challenged, and governed.
Native Network & NUSD
Native-network and digital-asset infrastructure. The recorded native-chain issuance is 1.5 quadrillion NUSD, with zero burned in the referenced mint record. Token issuance is not equivalent to cash reserves, market capitalization, redemption liquidity, or guaranteed asset value.

Artificial general intelligence and superintelligence are long-term research aspirations, not demonstrated Numina capabilities. Planned capabilities are not represented as completed deployments.
