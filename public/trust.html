TRUST CENTER / ENTERPRISE REVIEW

The evidence behind an operating decision.

Evaluate Numina through documented controls, release records and clearly stated operating boundaries. Use these resources to prepare a security, technical or procurement review.

Request an evidence reviewRead security documentation

01 / REVIEW RESOURCES

Find the evidence relevant to the decision.

Security and privacy

Review documented controls, data-handling terms, security reporting and the responsibilities that require deployment-specific agreement.

Software and release identity

Inspect artifact inventories, runtime status, build records and the difference between current and historical evidence.

Commercial and legal scope

Review service terms, software licensing, jurisdiction requirements and the boundaries of financial or charitable activity.

02 / EVIDENCE STANDARD

Match the claim to its source.

Documented capability
Published software and control descriptions document the stated implementation scope. Current operation and effectiveness require fresh verification. They do not establish a customer deployment.
Observed operation
A dated runtime response or test result establishes what was checked in that environment and observation window.
External evidence and assurance
External assessments, provider receipts and authorizations require their own records, identities and applicable scope.

03 / ASSURANCE STATUS

Set requirements before procurement acceptance.

No SOC 2 report, ISO 27001 certification, FedRAMP authorization, independent reserve audit or government accreditation is asserted here. Required assurances and outstanding evidence are reviewed against the actual engagement.

An enterprise review can cover architecture, data flows, administrative access, key management, incident responsibilities, recovery evidence and vendor dependencies. Contractual commitments require an accepted agreement.

Make the review specific.

Share the use case, deployment requirements and procurement criteria so the discussion can focus on the evidence your organization needs.
