EVIDENCE SCOPE

Different evidence answers
different questions.

A description, a test and an external receipt each have a role. Review the identity, date, environment and limitations that accompany the record.

Documented capability
States the implementation scope. Current operation and effectiveness require fresh verification.
Observed operation
A dated response or test describes what was checked in that environment and observation window.
External assurance
An assessment, authorization or provider receipt requires its own identity and applicable scope.
