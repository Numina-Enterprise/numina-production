01

Historically documented controls

NUMINA’s security documentation describes account separation, authorization, same-origin write checks, revision-bound journal records and bounded requests. Current operation and effectiveness require fresh verification.

02

Assurance status

No SOC 2 report, ISO 27001 certification, FedRAMP authorization, independent reserve audit or government accreditation is asserted here. Readiness work is separate from independent assessment.

03

Enterprise review

Request an architecture and responsibility review covering data flows, administrative access, key management, incident response, recovery evidence and vendor dependencies.

Scope the evidence review

  • Architecture, authorization boundaries and account isolation.
  • Data inventory, retention, deletion and international transfers.
  • Incident ownership, response procedures and tested recovery.
  • Independent assessments and precise production configuration.
  • Custody, settlement and reserve evidence for any proposed financial operation.
