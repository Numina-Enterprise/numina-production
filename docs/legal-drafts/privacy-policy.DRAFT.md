## Who we are and what this policy covers

We are **SK Consulting LLC, doing business as Numina Labs**, a consulting practice. This policy applies to information we collect when you visit numinalabs.xyz, contact us, or engage us for consulting work. Below we explain what we collect, how we use and share it, how long we keep it, and the choices you have.

## Information we collect

### Information you provide to us
- **Contact information** you send through the contact page or email: name, email, organisation, and your message.
- **Engagement information**: the scope of work, invoices and correspondence.
- **Compliance (KYC) information**, only when we need it before accepting an engagement or payment: legal name, any trade name, address, country, date of birth or incorporation date, the **last four digits only** of a tax ID, website, email, and for organisations, beneficial owners holding 25% or more and a control person. We do not ask for full Social Security or tax ID numbers.

### Information collected automatically
- **Server and security logs** by our hosting/CDN provider (for example IP address, browser type and request time), used for security and reliability. [ATTORNEY/OPS: confirm provider log retention.]
- We do not use advertising trackers. [OPS: confirm analytics, if any.]

### Information from other sources
- **Payment information from Stripe**, such as payment status, amount, the card's country and last four digits. Card numbers are handled by Stripe and are not stored by us.
- **Public government sanctions lists** (for example the U.S. Treasury OFAC SDN and consolidated lists) that we compare names against.

## How and why we use information
- To respond to you and to perform, invoice and support engagements.
- To meet legal obligations and manage risk: identity checks, sanctions screening and reviewing payments for unusual patterns (for example structuring or rapid refunds). These checks run on our own local systems; matches are reviewed by a person and are never decided automatically.
- To keep the website secure.

Where privacy laws require a legal basis, we rely on performance of a contract, compliance with legal obligations, and our legitimate interests in preventing fraud and sanctions violations. [ATTORNEY: confirm for applicable jurisdictions.]

## Sharing information
We do not sell personal information. We share it only:
- with **service providers** who help us operate (Stripe for payments; our hosting/CDN provider), under their terms;
- with a **licensed identity-verification provider**, if we engage one in future (we will update this policy first);
- with **legal advisers**, or with **authorities** when the law requires it (for example a sanctions report);
- in connection with a business transfer, subject to this policy.

## How long we keep information
We keep information only as long as needed for the purposes above or as the law requires:
- contact messages that do not lead to an engagement: about 12 months;
- engagement and payment records: as long as needed for tax and accounting (generally at least 7 years) [ATTORNEY: confirm];
- KYC and screening records: **5 years after the relationship ends**, then securely deleted [ATTORNEY: confirm];
- the screening audit log keeps identifiers and hashes only, not names.

## Security
KYC records are stored encrypted (AES-256-GCM) on systems we control and are not on the public website. Approvals require a signed human sign-off. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

## Your choices and rights
You may ask to access, correct or delete your information, or ask how it is used, by contacting us. Some records must be kept for legal reasons (for example screening records) and we will tell you if that applies. Depending on where you live you may have additional rights under laws such as the GDPR or U.S. state privacy laws; you may also appeal a decision on a request by replying to our response.
