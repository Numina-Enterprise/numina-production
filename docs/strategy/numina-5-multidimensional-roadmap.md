# NUMINA 5.0 — Multidimensional advancement charter

Status: PROPOSED / NOT DEPLOYED
Date: 2026-11-15
Business: SK Consulting LLC dba Numina Labs

## Canonical identity
NUMINA is the canonical codebase and source tree. Models, agents, applications, science, world data, trust, commerce, and infrastructure are governed branches, not competing roots. This strategic document does not modify existing release identities or signed evidence. The existing Numina 5.0.0 application/control release is distinct from the proposed Numina 5.0 LLM product family.

## Twelve workstreams
1. Intelligence: multiple evaluated model candidates and owned model improvements.
2. Reasoning: math, code, planning and calibration.
3. Memory: tenant-isolated, permissioned retrieval and retention.
4. Agency: bounded tools, human approval, and audit.
5. Perception: image, audio, geospatial and telemetry interfaces.
6. Science: reproducible experiments, simulations and research.
7. Applications: web, mobile, desktop, API and SDK.
8. Infrastructure: portable inference, observability and measured cost.
9. Trust: signed manifests, identity, provenance and release gates.
10. Commerce: subscriptions, metered API, private licensing and per-flavor profitability.
11. Organization: SK Consulting LLC dba Numina Labs, IP, governance and distribution.
12. Frontier: long-horizon research; superintelligence is aspirational, not established.

## Acceptance
Every checkpoint requires exact revision and weight digest, tokenizer, licensing and provenance, frozen benchmark rubric, held-out evaluation, security and tool-boundary tests, latency and throughput measurement, and economic viability. Missing evidence is NOT RUN or INCONCLUSIVE, never PASS.

## Financial and corporate boundary
Per-flavor contribution margin and fully allocated operating profit must be tracked separately. The 744 GPU-hour at $8/hour scenario is an estimate of $7,382.40 monthly including contingency, not spending approval. LLC membership interests are not publicly traded stock; equity financing or stock issuance requires separate legal and owner decisions.

## Non-negotiable controls
No production deployment, merge, cloud provisioning, treasury action, bridge release, access change, or signing is authorized here. Keep existing Authority + Guardian signing and independent owner approval gates. No model or agent may approve its own privileged actions. Existing security and acceptance blockers remain unresolved until evidenced.
