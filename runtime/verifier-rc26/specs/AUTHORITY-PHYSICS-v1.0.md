# Numina Authority Physics v1.0

Numina treats legitimate economic power as a bounded, derived field over economic state. Physics analysis is observational: metrics may constrain authority but never create it.

## Field laws

1. **Conservation** — no reachable risk-adding consequence may exceed legitimate upstream rights after reservations, commitments, uncertainty, encumbrance, and valid transformations.
2. **Causal precedence** — future consequence cannot bootstrap present authority.
3. **Horizon dependence** — MREC is a function of topology, activation delay, velocity, and time horizon.
4. **No cycle amplification** — graph cycles cannot manufacture apparent authority or MREC.
5. **Locality** — distributed authority must be explicitly partitioned/fenced or coordinated.
6. **Failure monotonicity** — faults may leave risk-adding authority unchanged or contract it, never expand it by uncertainty.
7. **Uncertainty occupancy** — unresolved reachable obligation consumes authority until evidence narrows the possibility set.
8. **Measurement separation** — physics metrics are derived observations and cannot themselves grant authority.

## Reference metrics

- Horizon MREC
- Authority liquidity by horizon
- Structural authority entropy index
- Shared-dependency concentration (integer basis points)
- Parallel-path authority gradient (integer basis points)
- Compromise edge min-cut

## Safety envelope

A `SafetyEnvelope` defines machine-verifiable upper/lower bounds over the derived metrics. `SafetyEnvelopeProof` binds both the envelope and exact metrics digests. A failed envelope does not mutate authority; it is an input to deterministic governance/contraction policy.

## Integer semantics

All reference quantities are integer units. Ratios use basis points. Floats are not accepted in constitutional/physics calculations.
