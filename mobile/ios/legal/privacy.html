Privacy policy.

01 / OPERATOR & SCOPE

Operator and processing responsibilities.

SK Consulting LLC operates Numina and determines the purposes of ordinary account, request, support and operational processing described here. When Numina processes a customer’s dataset solely on the customer’s instructions, the roles, permitted processing and safeguards must be set out in an accepted engagement and, where required, a data processing agreement. This notice does not itself execute that agreement.

This notice covers the current Numina workspace and its maintained source. Separate sign-in, payment, social media, model, cloud and connected-account services also have their own privacy notices and responsibilities. A self-hosted installation uses the hosts and integrations selected by its operator; it is not automatically covered by the hosted workspace’s provider arrangements. A customer operating a separate installation is responsible for its own users and data-processing configuration.

02 / INFORMATION & PURPOSE

What we collect and why.

Accounts and service requests

Sign-in supplies your account identifier and email. A service request stores the service selected, title, brief, success criteria, business country, request identifier, consent revision, timestamps, status and activity notes. Source and mutation hashes help prevent duplicate changes and preserve record integrity. We use these records to review and deliver the requested work, communicate with you, administer access and track scope, delivery and acceptance. Without the required account and request fields we cannot save or manage your request.

Payments and customer support

Stripe collects payment information at checkout. Numina retains matched checkout and payment identifiers, amounts, currency, payment status, webhook identifiers and hashes, and refund or dispute facts for reconciliation, fraud prevention, accounting and resolving complaints. The workspace does not store your full card number. A confirmed charge is not a bank payout or verified reserve. Email and support communications contain the details you choose to send and are used to answer you and manage the engagement.

Owner and control records

Authenticated control functions record principals, delegated grants, requests, proposed actions, review decisions, timestamps, evidence references and journal hashes. Owner-only financial functions store supporting PDF or image files, file names and hashes, and metadata such as legal entity, provider, relationship, jurisdictions, document reference, source URL, stated expiry date and review notes. Provider observations and receipt imports may contain account references, addresses, transaction hashes, amounts, currencies, status and verification results. These records support authorized operations, evidence review and reconciliation; storing a document does not establish that its issuer, ownership or legal coverage has been verified.

Do not upload passwords, unrelated customer records or unnecessary personal or sensitive information. Personal information included in a document remains personal information even when the document is private or identified by a hash.

Technical access and abuse prevention

Hosting and authentication infrastructure receives technical connection information, including IP addresses, browser and device information, request details and cookies needed to serve and protect the service. Application abuse counters derive a pseudonymous hash from an IP address with a time bucket, or from an account identifier and request method. These hashes are not a promise of anonymity. They are used to rate-limit abuse, not to create advertising profiles.

Current application telemetry records request counts, response times, status codes, generalized route categories and random request or instance identifiers. It excludes raw paths, query strings, account identifiers, request bodies and treasury values from the application telemetry payload. The process-local aggregate covers a rolling window of roughly five minutes; emitted logs and provider infrastructure logs may have different retention settings. Owner-requested circuit measurements use a bounded classical simulator; their results are returned to the requester and are not written into application telemetry or treasury records.

03 / DEVICE, COOKIES & AUTOMATION

Application data handling.

The installable web app requires an internet connection. Its current service worker clears older Numina application caches and fetches current pages and assets without an offline fallback. It does not save private API responses or payment records for offline use. Authentication cookies remain in your browser according to the sign-in method and its settings. Blocking required cookies may prevent sign-in or account functions.

The separately configured reviewer account stores a password digest in server configuration and uses an eight-hour Secure, HTTP-only session cookie. Per-minute sign-in counters are derived from a hashed IP address and old counters are removed on subsequent requests. Reviewer sessions use the same own-record access limits as ordinary customer sessions; checkout is live. Reviewer access can be disabled and its session key rotated.

The current public Help & FAQ runs in the page against published FAQ content. Your typed question is not sent to a model or saved as a support ticket; clearing or closing the page clears its page conversation. Choosing email support sends the information you compose through your email provider. Current browser wallet creation and encrypted-backup inspection run locally; the published tools do not upload your wallet password, seed phrase or private key. Wallet extensions and network providers may process public addresses, network queries and transaction-related information under their own policies. Never send private keys or recovery phrases to Numina support.

The reviewed workspace request API stores briefs and does not itself call an external model or implement a model-training pipeline. Connecting an external AI, agent or provider is a separate authorized integration; determine its data, retention and training settings before using it with sensitive material. The current website code has no advertising pixel, behavioral advertising integration or sale of personal-information feature. Numina does not sell workspace personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. The general website does not use a browser Do Not Track signal to change required account functions; there is no cross-context advertising sharing in this workspace to opt out of. Contact us about applicable opt-out or preference rights.

04 / ACCESS & RECIPIENTS

Who receives information.

Your service records are available to your authenticated account and the authorized Numina operator. Owner financial evidence is restricted to the authenticated owner through the application. The owner may authorize selected journal facts for public presentation. The public journal summary identifies its capture date, record timestamps, scope and source hashes; it does not expose raw journal pages, private bank or provider account bindings, customer records or credentials. Public release metadata uses filtered projections. Original restricted evidence downloads and account-specific verification modules require authenticated owner access. Authorized personnel and contracted service providers may access information as needed for the permitted purpose and under applicable access and contractual restrictions.

The hosted web application uses Sites/OpenAI sign-in and hosting integration, Cloudflare-backed application database and object storage, and Stripe for consulting checkout and configured financial-account observations. Google Fonts supplies typography on pages that request its hosted styles and font files; those requests disclose technical connection information to Google. Links to LinkedIn, X, Facebook and other external services are ordinary external destinations; interactions on those services follow their policies. GitHub supports software hosting, release automation and configured workflow authentication. Other runtime deployments or integrations, including a Vercel verifier deployment or a customer-selected model or RPC provider, use their actual enabled providers and must be reviewed for the information they process.

We disclose relevant information to providers needed to operate an authorized service, in response to a valid legal requirement, to protect lawful rights and address fraud or security incidents, or as part of an actual business transfer subject to applicable safeguards and notice. We do not authorize providers to use private workspace records for unrelated purposes merely because they provide an integration. Independent sign-in and payment providers may retain information for their own obligations.

Read the applicable provider notices: OpenAI, Cloudflare, Stripe, Google, GitHub and, if used for the deployment, Vercel.

05 / LAWFUL USE & LOCATIONS

Legal basis and international processing.

Where a law requires a lawful basis, requested account and engagement processing relies on taking steps at your request and performing the applicable contract; security and service administration rely on legitimate operational interests balanced against your rights; necessary accounting, fraud, legal and dispute records may be processed to meet legal obligations or establish and defend claims. Optional processing that legally requires consent needs a separate, specific choice that can be withdrawn. Reading this policy is not blanket consent to every use.

Numina is operated from the United States. Hosting, sign-in, payment, support and connected services may process information in the United States and other countries used by the configured provider. Access from another country does not guarantee local hosting or legal eligibility. Before an engagement requires personal-data processing in a new region, the parties must determine the actual processing locations, applicable provider agreements, any required representative and lawful international-transfer arrangement. This notice does not claim that a transfer assessment, signed standard contractual clauses or a data-localization arrangement already exists for every country.

Special-category health data, government identifiers, biometric data, precise location, children’s data, financial credentials or regulated production datasets are not requested in ordinary intake. Agree the specific lawful processing and safeguards before transferring such material. The service is intended for adults 18 and older. Contact us if you believe a child’s personal information has been provided so we can review and remove it where required.

06 / RETENTION & SECURITY

Retention and security.

Workspace records persist across sessions. Service records and communications are retained while needed for the requested work, account administration and applicable obligations. Supporting files and evidence records remain available for owner review; a correction creates a new record and does not automatically erase the prior file. Transaction, tax, refund, dispute, fraud and security records may require longer retention for the particular obligation or claim.

Retention depends on the record, purpose, relevant legal duty and any agreed engagement schedule. This notice does not claim that every provider log, backup or record has a configured automatic deletion period. On a deletion or retention request we will identify the relevant records, required exception and retention reason, delete or deidentify information no longer needed, and include processors holding associated data on Numina’s behalf. Any retained data will remain restricted to the permitted reason; backup and independent-provider handling will be explained in the response.

The maintained application uses authenticated access checks, private/no-store responses for account data, secure session settings, request limits and record-integrity checks. No system can guarantee absolute security. Report a suspected incident through the security page or the contact address. We will investigate and provide legally required notifications; any engagement-specific incident commitments must be agreed in its contract.

07 / YOUR RIGHTS & ACCOUNT DELETION

Request access, correction or deletion.

Delete your Numina account and data

You can request deletion without installing the app or signing in. Email skott34@numinalabs.xyz with the subject “Delete my Numina account”. Send from your Numina account email where possible; if you cannot access it, contact the same address for help verifying ownership. Do not send passwords, payment-card numbers or identity documents in the initial message.

This request covers your Numina account association and associated personal information, including email, service-request titles and briefs, success criteria, country and activity notes. You may use the same address to request confirmation of processing, access, a copy or portability, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, withdrawal of consent, an applicable opt-out or limit on sensitive-data use, or review of an automated decision. Rights and exceptions depend on applicable law; using the contact route does not require creating another account.

SK Consulting LLC handles the request. Sending an email starts the process and does not instantly delete records. We will use proportionate verification, respond within the applicable legal deadline, explain any lawful extension or refusal and the data retained and why, and give any available appeal route. You may reply with “Privacy appeal” to request review of a refusal, authorize a representative where the law allows, and complain to the relevant data-protection authority or attorney general. We will not unlawfully discriminate against you for exercising privacy rights.

For EU/EEA or UK rights where applicable, the ordinary response period is one month, with only lawful extensions and notice. For covered California requests it is generally 45 days, with only lawful extensions; for covered Iowa chapter 715D requests it is 90 days with the permitted extension and appeal process. A shorter applicable deadline controls. These statements do not declare that every law applies to every engagement.

To complain about how we handle your information, email the same address with “Privacy complaint” and describe the issue. No special wording or account is required. We will acknowledge privacy complaints promptly and, where UK data protection law applies, within 30 days; investigate without undue delay, keep you informed and explain the outcome. You can then raise an unresolved concern with the UK Information Commissioner or another relevant supervisory authority. The complaint acknowledgment does not extend a separate rights-request deadline.

Deleting your Numina association does not delete your independent ChatGPT or payment-provider account, public blockchain records, or information an independent provider must lawfully retain. Signing out, uninstalling, closing a local wallet tool or deleting a downloaded backup does not delete server-side records. If you act as a customer’s user and your employer controls the dataset, we may coordinate the request with that controller as legally required.
