03 / REDISTRIBUTION & PROVIDERS

Redistribution requirements and notice gaps.

Retain upstream copyright, license, attribution and notice files. The solc-js JavaScript wrapper declares MIT, while the bundled Solidity compiler retains its GPL license; they are recorded as separate components. For GPL, LGPL, MPL or another source-related license, review the exact distribution, modifications, linking and corresponding-source obligations before supplying binaries. A license inventory alone does not satisfy every obligation. Native libraries bundled in a Python wheel or another binary need their own artifact-level review when that binary is distributed.

Development packages and an upstream source archive are not automatically shipped to website visitors. A dependency declaration is not a service activation or provider authorization. Cloud hosting, sign-in, payments, model usage, provider APIs and remotely supplied Google Fonts have their own agreements and terms. Font-family declarations or an integration reference do not mean every provider SDK or model weight has been bundled or licensed by Numina.

The exact notice files and package paths are recorded in the inventory. Some publisher packages supply only a short license declaration or an incomplete notice. Those gaps are explicitly listed; obtain the complete publisher notice or exclude the affected development package from a distributable before redistributing it. This page does not claim an independent legal certification of every host, integration or future package.
